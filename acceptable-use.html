Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

Purpose

This policy outlines acceptable and responsible use of G4L IT resources. It ensures lawful, ethical, and secure operations across the network and devices managed by the organisation.

Scope

This applies to all staff, students, contractors and guests who use G4L computing resources.

Policy Highlights

  • No unauthorised access to systems or data.
  • Use only licensed software and follow copyright rules.
  • Protect credentials and do not share accounts.
  • Report security incidents to the Service Desk immediately.
