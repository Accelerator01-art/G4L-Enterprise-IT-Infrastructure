Service Desk / Help Desk Procedures

Contact the IT Service Desk for account issues, system access, and incident reporting.

How to Raise a Ticket

  1. Email: support@g4l.edu (include device details & description)
  2. Provide screenshots and classification (P1/P2/P3)
  3. Tickets triaged Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00

Service Levels (SLA)

  • P1 Critical: Response within 15 minutes, target resolution 4 hours
  • P2 Major: Response within 1 hour, target same business day
  • P3 Minor: Response within 4 business hours
