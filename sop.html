Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Procedures for routine operations, account provisioning, and daily lab checks.

Daily Lab Start-up

  1. Power on lab devices and verify network connectivity
  2. Run health-checks and verify backups completed
  3. Review open tickets and assign to Lab Support

Account Provisioning

Provision accounts via Service Desk ticket with role, duration and access level details. Apply GPOs and log provisioning in the register.
