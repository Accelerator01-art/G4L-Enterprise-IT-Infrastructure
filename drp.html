Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)

Purpose: Ensure continuity and recovery of critical IT services after a disruptive event.

Objectives

  • Minimize downtime for critical systems
  • Define RTO and RPO targets
  • Establish communication and escalation paths

Backup & Restore

Backups are taken daily for critical systems, with off-site retention. RPO = 24 hours, RTO = 24 hours for core services.
